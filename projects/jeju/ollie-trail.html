JEJU OLLE TRAIL

Alleyways

NATURE, CULTURE AND COMMUNITY

Olle means a narrow alley in the old Jeju dialect. Each walking course is 10-18km on average through the coasts, mountains, and villages. There are 27 routes and 437km of trail.
