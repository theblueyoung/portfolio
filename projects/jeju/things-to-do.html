Discover the real Jeju

HALLA MOUNTAIN

Halla mountain is at the centre of Jeju island, it is the highest mountain in South Korea at 1,950 meters (6,398 feet). There are over 4,000 animal species, and spring and autumn is the best season to visit.

SUNGSAN ILLCHUL-BONG

Seongsan Ilchulbong is also known as 'Sunrise peak' and was formed more than 100,000 years ago by volcanic eruptions. UNESCO recognised it as a world natural heritage in 2007, and it is an extraordinary place to watch the sunrise.

JUSANGJEOLLI CLIFF

Jusangjeolli Cliff is a hexagonal rock formed by lava from the Halla mountain eruption. These stone pillars piled up and created a unique natural scenery.

HAENYEO SCHOOL

One of the iconic figures of Jeju is the haenyeo. Jeju Haenyeo School was opened in 2007, and they help preserve haenyeo culture by offering diving experiences to visitors.
